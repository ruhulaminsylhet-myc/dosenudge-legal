DoseNudge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

DoseNudge is developed by Ruhul Amin (Milton Keynes, UK).

Data storage

All medication names, dosages, schedules, and adherence history you enter into DoseNudge are stored locally on your device only, using Android's local app storage. This data is never transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by us or any third party. We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers.

If you uninstall the app, this data is permanently deleted.

Advertising

DoseNudge is funded by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and other technical data to serve and measure relevant ads. This collection is performed by Google, not by DoseNudge directly. You can review and control Google's ad personalisation settings in your device's Google Settings app, and see Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifications

DoseNudge schedules local reminder notifications on your device to remind you when to take your medication. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on-device and are not sent through any external push notification service.

Children

DoseNudge is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a new "last updated" date.
